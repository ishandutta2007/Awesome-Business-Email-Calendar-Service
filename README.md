## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms



The business email and calendar market is dominated by a handful of hyperscale providers, with per-user monthly pricing as the standard model. Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 lead in market share and ecosystem integration, while specialized providers like Fastmail and Proton Mail differentiate on privacy and open standards. Zimbra remains the most prominent open-core alternative, offering both subscription and perpetual licensing models . Open-Xchange powers many white-label hosted email services, including ServeTheWorld and CloudPro.ng, with plans starting at approximately NOK 49/month (about $5) and NGN 3,483/month respectively .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[Zimbra Collaboration](https://github.com/Zimbra/zm-build)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Zimbra/zm-build?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Zimbra/zm-build/stargazers)  

  **The most widely deployed open-source email and collaboration suite**, GPL-2.0 / CPAL-1.0 licensed. **Full-featured groupware** with email, calendar, contacts, tasks, and file sharing. Web-based interface with Outlook Connector for native Outlook synchronization . Supports LDAP and Active Directory authentication, 2FA, and S/MIME encryption. **Used by enterprises and educational institutions worldwide**, particularly in Japan . Available in Open Source Edition (free) and commercial editions with ActiveSync, backup, and support .



- **[SOGo](https://github.com/Alinto/sogo)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Alinto/sogo?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Alinto/sogo/stargazers)  

  **Scalable OpenGroupware.org**, LGPL-2.1 licensed. **Focus on simplicity and scalability** with native support for CalDAV, CardDAV, GroupDAV, and ActiveSync . **Microsoft Outlook compatibility** through OpenChange, allowing SOGo to emulate an Exchange server for Outlook clients . Web interface, Thunderbird Lightning, Apple Calendar, and mobile device support. **SOGo 6 in development** with modern UI, admin console, and scheduled email sending . Actively maintained by Alinto since 2022 .



- **[grommunio (Gromox)](https://github.com/grommunio/gromox)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/grommunio/gromox?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/grommunio/gromox/stargazers)  

  **Drop-in replacement for Microsoft Exchange**, open-source. **RPC/HTTP (Outlook Anywhere), MAPI/HTTP, EWS, IMAP, POP3** — full Exchange protocol compatibility . **grommunio Web** provides web-based groupware with email, calendar, contacts, tasks, and notes. **S/MIME and OpenPGP** for email signing and encryption with keys never leaving the browser . Includes extensions for **grommunio Files** (Nextcloud, ownCloud, Seafile, WebDAV with OnlyOffice editing), Meet, Chat, and mobile device management. **80+ languages**, command palette, keyboard shortcuts, and progressive web app support . Scalable to tens of thousands of concurrent sessions .



- **[Nextcloud Groupware](https://github.com/nextcloud)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nextcloud/server?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/nextcloud/server/stargazers)  

  **Email, calendar, and contacts on your own infrastructure**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Nextcloud Mail** with multiple accounts, threaded conversations, and delegation features . **Nextcloud Calendar** with free-busy checks, resource booking, and delegation . **Nextcloud Contacts** with organization charts, groups, and sharing . **Microsoft 365 integration** on paid tiers: Teams file sharing, calendar and contact sync . Requires Nextcloud Files as a base, licensed per user with 50-user minimum for commercial .



- **[Group-Office](https://github.com/Intermesh/groupoffice)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Intermesh/groupoffice?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Intermesh/groupoffice/stargazers)  

  **Enterprise CRM and groupware platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Email, calendars, files, CRM, and project management** in one browser-based platform . **Collabora Online integration now free** in Community package (moved from paid tier in 26.0) — edit Office and OpenDocument files collaboratively in browser . **New in 26.0**: Kanban module, time approval in Projects, forms module (beta), and rebuilt Notes/Bookmarks/Comments on GOUI framework . Syncs with desktop/mobile via IMAP, CalDAV, CardDAV. **Pricing**: from €12/month for 1 user to €400 for 100 users, 30-day free trial .



- **[Kurrier](https://github.com/kurrier-org/kurrier)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/kurrier-org/kurrier?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/kurrier-org/kurrier/stargazers)  

  **Modern self-hosted workspace for email, calendars, contacts, and storage**, open-source. **Connect any email provider**: IMAP, SMTP, SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, Postmark . **CalDAV calendar sync** compatible with iOS, macOS, Android (DAVx⁵), and Thunderbird . **Full CardDAV support** for contacts synchronization . **Generic OIDC/SSO support** for identity provider integration . **Management API** for programmatic provisioning . **Message Inspector** for debugging email structure . **Kurrier Drive** with WebDAV/S3 storage . Built with **Next.js, TypeScript, Nitro, PostgreSQL**. Docker-ready .



- **[Citadel](https://github.com/citadel/citadel)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/citadel/citadel?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/citadel/citadel/stargazers)  

  **Lightweight groupware including email, calendar, address books, forums, mailing lists, IM, wiki, and blog**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Self-contained** — one installation provides complete collaboration environment. C-based, efficient for small to medium deployments .



- **[Tine](https://github.com/tine-groupware/tine)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/tine-groupware/tine?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/tine-groupware/tine/stargazers)  

  **Digital collaboration platform for companies and organizations**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **Groupware functionalities plus add-ons** for team collaboration. Docker deployment available .



- **[EGroupware](https://github.com/EGroupware/egroupware)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/EGroupware/egroupware?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/EGroupware/egroupware/stargazers)  

  **Full-featured groupware suite**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Calendars, address books, notepad, project management, CRM, knowledge management, wiki, and CMS** . PHP-based, mature and extensible .



- **[Roundcube](https://github.com/roundcube/roundcubemail)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/roundcube/roundcubemail?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/roundcube/roundcubemail/stargazers)  

  **Browser-based multilingual IMAP client**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most widely adopted open-source webmail** . MIME support, address book, folder manipulation, message searching, and spell checking. **Often paired with SOGo or Zimbra** as the front-end webmail .



- **[mailcow: dockerized](https://github.com/mailcow/mailcow-dockerized)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mailcow/mailcow-dockerized?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/mailcow/mailcow-dockerized/stargazers)  

  **Mail server suite with modern Web UI**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Based on Dovecot, Postfix, SOGo, Rspamd** and other open-source components . Provides complete email server infrastructure with calendar and contacts via SOGo. Docker-based deployment.



- **[Carbonio Community Edition](https://github.com/zextras/carbonio-ce)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/zextras/carbonio-ce?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/zextras/carbonio-ce/stargazers)  

  **Free and open-source email and collaboration platform by Zextras**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Complete email solution** for organizations seeking digital sovereignty through on-premises self-hosted servers . Built by the team behind Zimbra's original development.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Zimbra licensing**: Perpetual licenses bind to a specific major version; accessing updates beyond that version requires additional maintenance agreements. Subscription licenses include updates and support but may cost more over the long term . **Zimbra Open Source Edition** lacks some advanced features and support options available in commercial editions .

- **Nextcloud Groupware** requires Nextcloud Files as a base platform. Commercial licensing starts at **50 users** with per-user annual fees .

- **Group-Office 26.0** moved Collabora Online integration from the paid tier to the Community package, making collaborative document editing free for all users .

- Open-source groupware platforms (Zimbra, SOGo, grommunio, Nextcloud Groupware) provide self-hosted ownership and transparency, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees, 24/7 support, and managed infrastructure remain primarily commercial offerings. **Email infrastructure requires expertise** in DNS, spam filtering, TLS, and backup procedures. 📧
